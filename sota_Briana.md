## Existing Applications

Among the applications that align with the project idea and contain relevant functionalities are:

- [Buoy Health](https://www.buoyhealth.com/)
- [Symptomate](https://symptomate.com/)
- [Mayo Clinic Symptom Checker](https://www.mayoclinic.org/symptom-checker/select-symptom/itt-20009075)
- [Infermedica](https://infermedica.com/)
- [Ada Health](https://ada.com/)

These utilize AI to assist users in diagnosing potential health conditions based on symptom input.

### Key Features Observed in These Existing Apps

- Analyze the symptoms provided by the user to identify possible health conditions 
- Users are guided through a series of questions to refine the diagnosis 
- Based on the analysis, the applications suggest potential conditions and recommend next steps for treatment 

### Interaction Methods

- Some use chatbots to engage users in a conversational manner 
- Others rely on forms to collect information 
- Some integrate both chatbots and forms to enhance user experience and improve the accuracy of symptom analysis

## Datasets for Disease Prediction

To support disease prediction, the following datasets are relevant:

### General Disease Datasets

- **[MIMIC-IV](https://physionet.org/content/mimiciv/3.1/)**  
  One of the most well-known clinical datasets, containing comprehensive data from ICU patients. It includes diagnoses, procedures, vital signs, medications administered and other relevant clinical information. Access is provided through AWS.

- **[Disease Symptoms and Patient Profile Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/uom190346a/disease-symptoms-and-patient-profile-dataset)**  
  This dataset links patient profiles to symptoms and enables researchers to build models that predict diseases based on symptom patterns.

- **[SymbiPredict](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/dv5z3v2xyd/1)**  
  Similar to the above, it links symptoms to diseases for disease prediction.

- **[Disease Prediction Using Machine Learning](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/kaushil268/disease-prediction-using-machine-learnin)**  
  Similar concept, predicting multiple diseases based on symptoms.

### Heart Disease Datasets

- **[Heart Attack Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/waqi786/heart-attack-dataset)**  
  Contains data on patients who have experienced heart attacks, including key risk factors such as age, cholesterol levels and blood pressure.

- **[Heart Disease Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/johnsmith88/heart-disease-dataset)**  
  Provides information about the presence of heart disease in patients, along with features such as age, sex, chest pain type and resting blood pressure.

### X-ray Datasets

- **[Congenital Heart Disease X-ray Data](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/congenital-heart-disease/data)**  
  This dataset includes labeled X-ray images used for diagnosing congenital heart disease.

- **[NIH Chest X-ray Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/nih-chest-xrays/data/data)**  
  A large collection of labeled chest X-rays used for detecting various conditions.
