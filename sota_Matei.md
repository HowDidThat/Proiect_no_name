# Related research papers

Early detection of heart disease is crucial because it allows for timely intervention, potentially preventing serious complications such as heart attacks or strokes. Lifestyle changes, medications, or surgeries can be used to manage the disease if identified early. However, symptoms like chest pain or shortness of breath often appear only after the disease has progressed. 

Developing accurate models to detect heart disease is essential because many cases are asymptomatic or have vague symptoms. Accurate prediction models can help in early diagnosis, reducing mortality and improving patient outcomes. With advances in machine learning and artificial intelligence, we can create predictive algorithms that analyze complex patterns in data to detect heart disease more effectively.

# Application use-cases
 - Symptom Checker & Diagnosis Simulation: A medical student wants to practice diagnosing heart diseases by inputting symptoms and clinical findings to simulate real-world cases.
 - Case Study Practice Mode: A student wants to practice with pre-loaded or user-generated case studies to test their diagnostic skills.
 - Laboratory Result Analysis: A student wants to practice interpreting lab results to diagnose heart disease.
